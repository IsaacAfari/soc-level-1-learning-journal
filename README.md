# soc-level-1-learning-journal
My hands-on cybersecurity learning journey through TryHackMe SOC Level 1.
A curated collection of selected TryHackMe's SOC level 1 rooms and exercises from my cybersecurity learning journey.

I started using TryHackMe in as a hands-on way to build and strengthen my practical cybersecurity knowledge. Since then, I have worked through a range of rooms across SOC operations.

This repository does not contain every room I have completed. Instead, I selectively document rooms and exercises that I find particularly useful, interesting, or representative of the areas I have explored.

What I Document
The public repository is intentionally selective.

A room may be included because it:

introduced an important security concept;
provided meaningful hands-on practice;
demonstrated a technique or tool I want to remember and share;
complemented other work in my portfolio; or
was particularly interesting or challenging.
Other completed rooms may remain undocumented here. They may instead be kept as personal learning notes or revisited later.

Learning Notes
I maintain separate private notes for more detailed learning material, including experimentation, troubleshooting, and things I am still working to understand.

The public repository is therefore intended to be a curated portfolio of selected work, rather than a complete record of everything I have completed on TryHackMe.

Ongoing
My TryHackMe learning is ongoing. As I continue exploring different areas of cybersecurity, I may add more selected rooms and exercises to this repository.
